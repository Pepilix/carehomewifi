Technology & integration

Connecting the technology modern care depends on.

Care management tools, AI-assisted monitoring, nurse call, staff devices and building systems all rely on the network underneath them. We design that network around their requirements and work through the technical detail with each supplier.

Discuss a Technology Project

What each system needs

Different systems, different demands on the network.

A network that's fine for guest WiFi can still fail a monitoring sensor in a resident's room. We plan for each type of system explicitly.

  • Care management & digital records

    Reliable coverage wherever care is recorded, and staff devices that roam between access points without dropping.

  • AI-assisted monitoring

    Consistent in-room coverage, devices on their own secured segment, and a dependable internet connection where alerts are processed in the cloud.

  • Nurse call & alerts

    Priority on the network and resilience at the internet edge, agreed with the nurse-call supplier.

  • Staff devices & voice

    Clean roaming and quality-of-service for calls and messaging as staff move through the building.

  • Building systems

    Access control, CCTV and building management isolated from residents and guests.

  • Residents & visitors

    Good everyday connectivity for calls, streaming and smart TVs — without competing with care systems.

Our role

Clear responsibilities, so nothing falls between suppliers.

Care technology projects go wrong when everyone assumes the network is someone else's problem. We're explicit about where our responsibility starts and ends.

We are responsible for

  • The internet connection, backup and failover
  • WiFi coverage and capacity where the system is used
  • Switching, cabling and power over Ethernet for connected devices
  • Network segmentation, security rules and priority
  • Testing connectivity with the supplier before go-live
  • Network troubleshooting once the system is in use

The technology supplier is responsible for

  • Their hardware, software and its configuration
  • Clinical use, alerting workflows and staff training on their system
  • Their system's compliance and data handling
  • Providing network requirements and supporting joint testing

We don't supply, configure, certify or take clinical responsibility for third-party care software.

How we work with suppliers

A simple, repeatable process.

  1. Gather requirements

    We get the supplier's network requirements in writing — coverage, bandwidth, ports, services and how devices connect.

  2. Design & configure

    We design the network segment and rules for the system and configure them before devices arrive.

  3. Test together

    We test connectivity with the supplier on site before the system goes live.

  4. Support

    If problems arise later, we investigate the network side and work with the supplier to resolve them.
