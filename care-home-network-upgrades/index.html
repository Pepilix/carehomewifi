What we look at

What often stays, and what usually needs to change.

Structured cabling

Often reusable if it was installed to a reasonable standard, even where hardware needs replacing.

Cabinets & containment

Frequently fine as-is, particularly where space and power allow for upgraded equipment.

Access point hardware

Sometimes reusable with reconfiguration; sometimes genuinely due for replacement if outdated or underpowered.

Network segmentation

Usually needs correcting — flat, unsegmented networks are one of the most common underlying problems we find.

Switching & PoE budget

Frequently insufficient for current device counts and needs upgrading to support the network properly.
