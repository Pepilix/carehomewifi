Service

WiFi surveys and network design for care homes.

Every good network starts with an accurate picture of the building and how it will be used. We measure rather than guess — whether we're diagnosing an existing home or designing a new one from the drawings.

Request a WiFi Assessment Discuss a New-Build Design

Three kinds of survey

The right survey for where the building is.

Design survey

For new builds and extensions. We plan access point positions, cabling and switching from the drawings, accounting for wall construction, room layouts and expected device numbers.

Diagnostic survey

For existing homes with problems. We measure coverage and performance on site, review the network configuration and comms room, and identify the causes of dropouts and dead spots.

Validation survey

After installation or an upgrade. We confirm that coverage, roaming and failover meet the design before handover, so the operator starts from a known-good network.

What we look at

More than signal strength.

Most care home WiFi problems aren't caused by weak signal alone. A useful survey looks at the whole chain, from the incoming line to the device in a resident's room.

  • Coverage in bedrooms, corridors, lounges, staff areas and outdoor spaces
  • Capacity — how many devices each access point is carrying, and when
  • Interference from neighbouring networks and building materials
  • Roaming — whether devices hand over cleanly as staff move around
  • Switching, PoE capacity and the condition of the comms room
  • How the network is segmented and secured
  • The internet connection, and whether there's a backup
  • Documentation — or the lack of it

Network design

A design built around how a care home actually works.

The output is a network specification that a contractor can build and an operator can live with.

Wireless

Access point numbers and positions for coverage and capacity, placed so they work architecturally as well as technically in residents' rooms and communal spaces.

Segmentation & security

Separate networks for care and operations, staff, residents, guests, building systems, security and voice — each with its own priority and access rules.

Switching & backbone

Core and edge switching, PoE budgets sized for the device count, and a fibre backbone between floors or wings where needed.

Internet resilience

Fibre as the primary supply, with a Starlink or 5G backup and automatic failover so the home stays connected if the line goes down.

What you receive

A clear report, not a sales pitch.

  • Summary of findings in plain English
  • The causes behind the problems you're seeing
  • What to keep, reconfigure or replace — and why
  • Recommended design and equipment
  • Options for phasing the work
