## The requirement

Roche Care Homes' existing WiFi was struggling to keep up. Inconsistent coverage was affecting staff use of digital health-monitoring tools, dropouts were interrupting residents' calls with family, and the network simply hadn't been built for the number of devices now in daily use by staff, residents and visitors.

## Our scope

- A survey of the existing infrastructure in each home
- A network design covering the whole of each property — communal areas, private rooms and outdoor spaces
- Installation of new access points and network equipment, cloud-managed from one place
- Staff training at handover
- A 12-month on-site support warranty

## Delivery

The homes were occupied throughout, so the work was rolled out in phases and planned around the care routine of each building rather than the other way round. Each phase was completed and tested before the next began.

## The result

Reliable coverage across the homes for staff devices, residents and visitors, a network that can be seen and managed centrally, and a single point of contact for support. Roche's development director described the work as completed "with minimal disruption to the site".
