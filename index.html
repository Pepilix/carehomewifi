Two routes in. One specialist team.

New-build care homes

Designed into the project from the outset.

For developers, construction directors and main contractors.

We work with developers and construction teams to deliver network infrastructure designed into the project from the outset — specified early, coordinated with the other trades, and handed over tested and documented.

  • Network specification and data point schedules at design stage
  • Coordination with M&E consultants, electrical contractors and the incoming-line provider
  • Installation, commissioning and testing to the construction programme
  • A consistent specification you can repeat across every development

Existing care homes

Find the real problem. Fix it properly.

For care home owners, managers and operations teams.

From unreliable WiFi to complete network upgrades, we identify the problem and deliver practical solutions while minimising disruption to residents and staff.

  • An on-site survey and diagnosis before anything is recommended
  • Keep the equipment that works; replace only what's holding the network back
  • Work phased around residents, staff shifts and care routines
  • Separate, prioritised networks for care, staff, residents and guests

After handover

Our involvement doesn't have to end at handover.

Networks change as homes fill up, devices are added and care technology evolves. We stay involved so problems are caught early and every home in an estate is supported the same way.

  • Remote monitoring of the core network and access points
  • Troubleshooting by engineers who know the building
  • Planned changes as devices and systems are added
  • Consistent configuration and documentation across multiple homes
