For developers, construction directors & main contractors

Network infrastructure for new-build care homes.

We work with developers and construction teams to deliver network infrastructure designed into the project from the outset — specified early, coordinated with the other trades, installed to the programme and handed over tested and documented.

Why early involvement matters

Getting the network right during construction costs far less than fixing it afterwards.

Once walls are closed and ceilings are in, moving a data point, adding containment or finding space for a larger cabinet becomes disruptive and expensive. Most of the WiFi problems we're asked to fix in existing homes trace back to decisions — or omissions — made at design stage.

Bringing us in early means the network is treated as part of the building: data points where access points actually need to be, enough switching and power for the device count, a properly sized comms room and an incoming line ordered in time.

What goes wrong when the network is an afterthought

  • Access point positions chosen for convenience, not coverage
  • No cabling to rooms that later need monitoring or nurse-call devices
  • Comms rooms too small, too hot or without suitable power
  • The incoming fibre ordered too late for opening day
  • No backup connection if that single line fails

How we work on a new build

From design coordination to handover.

  • Design coordination

    We produce the network specification and data point schedule, and work through it with the M&E consultant, architect and electrical designer — access point positions, containment routes, comms room size, power and cooling.

  • First fix

    Data points, containment and back boxes go in with the electrical first fix, to our schedule. Getting this stage right is what makes the rest of the installation quick and predictable.

  • Contractor interfaces

    We agree who does what with the main contractor and each trade — electrical, fire, nurse call, access control, AV — and coordinate the incoming line with the provider, so nothing falls between scopes.

  • Structured cabling

    Cabling and fibre backbone installed by us or by the site electrician to our specification, terminated, labelled and tested before the active equipment arrives.

  • Commissioning

    We install and configure the cabinet, switching, firewall, failover connection and access points, then test coverage, roaming and failover across the building.

  • Handover

    As-built documentation, configuration records and a walkthrough for the operator's team, with the option of ongoing support once residents move in.

What we design in

A resilient connection and a segmented network, from day one.

The same principles go into every new home we specify, adjusted to the building and the operator's systems.

  • Fibre as the primary internet supply, with a 5G or Starlink backup assessed for each site
  • Core switching and a fibre backbone sized for the device count, with headroom
  • Separate networks for care, staff, residents, guests and building systems
  • Coverage planned into residents' rooms, ready for monitoring and nurse-call devices
