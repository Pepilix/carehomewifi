Services

Care home network services, from specification to support.

We can take responsibility for one stage or the whole infrastructure journey — including the coordination with internet providers, contractors and care technology suppliers in between.

  • WiFi Surveys & Network Design

    Measured surveys of existing homes, designs for new builds from the drawings, and validation once installation is complete. Covers wireless, switching, segmentation and internet resilience.

    Surveys & design

  • Network Installation & Infrastructure

    Fibre incoming line and backup, comms cabinets, switching, fibre backbone, structured cabling and access points — installed, labelled, tested and documented.

    Installation

  • Managed WiFi & Support

    Monitoring, troubleshooting, updates and changes once the home is open, with consistent support across multi-site groups.

    Managed WiFi

  • Care Technology & Integration

    Making sure the network meets the requirements of care management, monitoring, nurse call and building systems, and coordinating with each supplier.

    Technology & integration
