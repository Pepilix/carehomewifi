## The requirement

LNT Care Developments builds care homes as a programme, not as one-off projects. They needed a network design that would cover each complete building for residents and staff, could be specified before construction rather than retrofitted, and could be repeated home after home without being reinvented each time.

## Our scope

- A standard WiFi and data network design used across LNT homes
- Data point schedules and specification for the site electrical contractor
- UniFi WiFi 7 access points on a fibre backbone, sized so each home gets the most out of its incoming supply
- Supply, installation, configuration and commissioning of the active network
- Coordination with LNT's contractors on timing, and with Ripple Solutions, who arrange the incoming line

## Delivery

Because the design is standard, the groundwork happens before we arrive. Data points are cabled, terminated and tested by the on-site electrical contractor to our schedule. Once the building is ready, two CareHomeWiFi engineers have a home fully up and running in a single working day — around 16 hours of installation time.

At Bradshaw Lodge that meant 28 access points and more than 150 data points covering 66 bedrooms, communal areas and staff spaces.

## The result

Each new LNT home opens with a complete, tested network built to the same standard as the last. The specification doesn't have to be re-argued on every project, installation fits neatly into the construction programme, and the homes are consistent to support afterwards.

LNT homes also use Sensio's AI-assisted monitoring in residents' rooms, which runs on the network we install. That's one reason in-room coverage is planned room by room rather than from the corridors.
