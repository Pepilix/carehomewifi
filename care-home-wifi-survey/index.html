Service

Care home WiFi surveys.

A survey tells you what's actually happening on a network, rather than what the coverage bars on a phone suggest. We run predictive surveys for buildings not yet built, and on-site diagnostic surveys for existing care homes.

Two kinds of survey, one clear outcome.

Predictive

For buildings not yet built.

Using architectural drawings, we model coverage, capacity and access point positioning before construction starts — so the design is right from first fix, not corrected after.

Diagnostic

For existing, operational buildings.

An on-site survey measures what's actually happening — signal strength, interference, roaming and channel utilisation — to explain problems a predictive model can't capture after the fact.

What's included

What a survey actually measures.

Heatmaps

Visual coverage mapping across every floor, room and corridor.

Signal strength

Measured, not estimated — identifying genuine dead spots and marginal coverage.

Interference

Other wireless sources and physical building materials that degrade signal quality.

Roaming behaviour

How devices hand over between access points as people move through the building.

Channel utilisation

Congestion across the wireless spectrum in use.

Capacity

Whether access points can support the number of devices actually connecting to them.

Every survey ends with a clear set of recommendations — not just data.
