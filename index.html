End-to-end capability

One team responsible for the whole infrastructure journey.

We don't hand over a drawing and walk away, or arrive on site with no context. The same team carries a project from specification to support — including the coordination with internet providers and other contractors that usually falls between the gaps.

  1. Design

    Coverage and capacity planning, network architecture, segmentation and a clear equipment specification.

  2. Coordinate

    Data point schedules for the electrician, containment routes, comms room requirements and incoming-line orders with the provider.

  3. Install

    Fibre backbone, switching, cabinets and access points installed to the programme or around the home's routine.

  4. Commission

    Configuration, failover and coverage testing, then as-built documentation and a proper handover.

  5. Support

    Monitoring, troubleshooting and changes once the building is occupied — by the people who built it.

How a care home network is put together

Resilient at the edge. Segmented inside.

Fibre is the primary internet supply, with a Starlink or 5G backup that takes over automatically if the line fails. Inside the building, care systems, staff devices, residents and guests each run on their own network.

Care technology & third-party systems

Connecting the technology modern care depends on.

Modern care homes rely on a growing range of network-connected systems — care management tools, AI-assisted monitoring, staff devices, nurse call and building infrastructure. Each has its own requirements. Our job is to make sure the network underneath meets them, and to work through the technical detail with each supplier.

  • Care management & records

    Digital care planning and records used on staff devices throughout the home.

  • AI-assisted monitoring

    Sensor-based monitoring in residents' rooms that depends on dependable in-room coverage.

  • Nurse call & alerts

    Call and alert systems where a dropped connection is not acceptable.

  • Staff devices & voice

    Tablets, handsets and phones that need to roam cleanly between access points.

  • Building systems

    Access control, CCTV, fire and building management systems on their own secured segment.

  • Resident connectivity

    Video calls with family, streaming and smart TVs — kept apart from operational traffic.

We have experience working alongside care technology providers including:

  • Sensio — AI-assisted monitoring and care technology
  • CPS — resident care systems
  • and other third-party care technology suppliers

We provide and support the connectivity these systems run on. We don't supply, configure or take clinical responsibility for third-party care software, and the names above do not imply a formal partnership.
